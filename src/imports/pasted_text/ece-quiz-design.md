Design and build a premium, futuristic animated ECE Technical Quiz web application.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT use a light theme, pink theme, pastel theme, generic education theme, or ordinary dashboard style.

The entire experience should feel like:
"Futuristic Night Sky + Electricity + Electronics + Thunder"

==================================================
1. VISUAL THEME
==================================================

Use a deep midnight/night-sky background.

Primary background:
- Almost-black navy
- Deep midnight blue
- Subtle blue/purple atmospheric gradients
- No white background

Create a cinematic night-sky environment throughout the application.

Background should contain:
- Hundreds of tiny glowing stars
- Different star sizes
- Subtle star twinkling animation
- A few brighter stars with soft glow
- Very subtle nebula/cloud effects
- Deep-space depth using multiple background layers

Do NOT make the background too busy.
The quiz content must remain highly readable.

==================================================
2. ELECTRIC / ECE VISUAL LANGUAGE
==================================================

Add futuristic electrical elements around the interface.

Use:
- Animated electric signal waves
- Oscilloscope-style waveform lines
- ECG-like signal patterns
- Sine waves
- Digital pulse signals
- Circuit traces
- Small glowing nodes
- Electronic connection lines
- Subtle PCB-inspired patterns

The electrical waves should slowly move across the background.

Some circuit lines should periodically light up like electricity is flowing through them.

Use a subtle electric-blue glow.

Add occasional lightning/thunder animation in the distant background.

Thunder animation should be:
- Very subtle
- Short
- Cinematic
- Rare
- Not distracting

When thunder occurs:
- Brief screen/background illumination
- Small electric particles
- Then return immediately to the dark night atmosphere

Do NOT make it look like a horror website.
